# Research Ownership and Contribution Policy

## Human Contribution and AI Assistance

This is a solo-human-owner, substantially AI-assisted research project. The human project owner supplied the overarching research objective and integrity constraints, requested critical reviews, and retains responsibility for public research claims and publication decisions. AI-assisted systems contributed substantially to method and protocol discussions, implementation and debugging, experiment execution, statistical and reproducibility checks, and research writing.

Project ownership does not imply that every algorithmic idea, technical decision, experiment, or line of code was independently produced without AI. The project does not make that claim.

## Human Research Responsibilities

The project records support the following bounded account of the owner's role:

- setting the overall research question around error-risk ranking for scientific claim verification;
- requiring leakage controls, frozen-result preservation, explicit evidence boundaries, and honest reporting of unfavorable or inconclusive results;
- requesting critical review and correction of provenance, label-isolation, statistical-language, licensing, and publication-quality problems;
- deciding whether the public claims, contribution statement, authorship, license, and release are acceptable;
- retaining responsibility for understanding and explaining the methods, results, limitations, and AI-assistance boundary.

These responsibilities are research direction and accountability. They are not evidence that the owner independently designed every feature, wrote every implementation, executed every command, or personally recomputed every statistic.

## AI-Assisted Technical Contributions

AI-assisted systems supported:

- literature discovery and citation checking;
- research-method and protocol discussions;
- code drafting, implementation, and debugging;
- experiment orchestration and execution;
- statistical analysis, leakage review, and reproducibility auditing;
- failure analysis and interpretation checks;
- documentation, figures, PDFs, portfolio drafts, and demo development;
- privacy, release-safety, and licensing checks.

AI assistance is not an author and does not own the work. Its substantial role must remain visible wherever the project is presented; public materials must not imply unaided human implementation.

## Individual Verification Policy

Personal technical understanding is marked verified only after the owner personally completes a dated task, records any assistance used, and retains the calculation, explanation, or code trace as evidence. AI-generated questions, reference criteria, or draft answers are preparation material, not owner evidence.

The private verification worksheet is currently:

`PENDING OWNER-PERFORMED VERIFICATION`

Uncompleted verification items remain `NOT VERIFIED`. Present-day verification can demonstrate current understanding, but it cannot retroactively establish unaided historical authorship.
