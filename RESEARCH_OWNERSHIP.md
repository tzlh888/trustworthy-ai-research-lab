# Research Ownership and Contribution Policy

## Project owner

The human project owner controls the research objective, protocol approvals, authorship, public claims, license choice, and release. The owner is responsible for understanding the methods, rerunning critical checks, reviewing citations, and explaining the limitations in interviews or applications.

Before publication, replace the neutral owner placeholder in `CITATION.cff` with the owner's chosen public identity. No personal identity is inferred from local filesystem or account metadata.

## AI-assisted work

AI-assisted tools supported:

- literature discovery and citation checking;
- code drafting and debugging;
- experiment orchestration under owner-approved protocols;
- statistical and leakage audits;
- documentation, figure, PDF, and demo generation;
- privacy and reproducibility checks.

AI assistance is not an author, does not own the work, and does not substitute for the owner's scientific responsibility. Public materials must not imply unaided implementation.

## Claims not made

This project does not claim a peer-reviewed publication, research supervisor, institutional affiliation, clinical validation, external replication, novel uncertainty theory, or general superiority of EARS. The working name EARS describes an experimental configuration.

## Third-party ownership

SciFact claims/annotations, corpus abstracts, upstream code, the pinned Hugging Face model, and software dependencies remain subject to their own licenses. They are not relicensed by this repository. Raw data and model weights are not included in the release candidate.
