# Archival Notice and Erratum - 14 October 2026

`EARS_selective_prediction_paper.md` and `EARS_selective_prediction_paper.pdf` are preserved as historical release artifacts. They are superseded by `Trustworthy_AI_Research_Lab_V2_Final.md` and its matching PDF.

The historical paper described the completed study period as "12 October 2026 - 15 November 2026" even though the archival PDF metadata records creation on 13 October 2026 and the latest correction was performed on 14 October 2026. Available records do not support presenting 15 November as an actual completed end date. The final paper therefore uses only chronology supported by public Git history and generated artifacts and explicitly qualifies earlier chronology.

The historical title and prose also used "preregistered." The project has evidence of internal protocol freezes and hash gates, but no verified third-party or publicly registered preregistration. The final manuscript uses "internally protocol-frozen" and distinguishes this from external preregistration, independent replication, and peer review.

The historical files are not edited or deleted because they document the prior public state. README and publication materials point to the corrected V2 manuscript as authoritative.
